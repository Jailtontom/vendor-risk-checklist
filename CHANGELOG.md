# Changelog

*Question numbers in each entry refer to that version's numbering.*

## 1.4 — November 2026
- Shortened the checklist from 50 to 42 questions by combining eight pairs of overlapping questions (v1.3: 3+6, 5+32, 16+17, 18+19, 15+23, 26+27, 40+41, 42+43). No topic was removed.
- Question 23 (v1.3 Q28) now also covers a practical risk appetite for outsourcing; question 24 (v1.3 Q29) now asks for a documented reason for each provider's risk level; the introduction to section 7 adds an internal check on AI outputs.
- Renumbered the questions. The wording of the questions refined with practitioner feedback in versions 1.2 and 1.3 (v1.3 numbers 4, 9, 31, 35, 38 and 46–50) is unchanged; in v1.4 they are 4, 8, 26, 29, 32 and 38–42.
- Added MAPPING.md with the full v1.3 → v1.4 table, and a "Number in v1.3" column to the CSV.
- Acknowledged practitioner feedback (Jonathan Blair, MISM, MMSC, independent risk management practitioner) as professional feedback, not endorsement.
- Published the PDF of version 1.4. The PDFs of versions 1.2 and 1.3 remain in the repository.

## 1.3 — November 2026
- Added section 7, "Providers that use AI" (questions 46–50): whether AI can act without staff approval, use of customer data for training and fourth parties, explainability and adverse-action reasons, testing for accuracy, bias and drift, and notice of material model changes with a fallback.
- Added an instruction before the section (request written answers and evidence; document limitations and who accepts the risk) and a note on regulatory scope (OCC Bulletin 2026-13, GAO-25-107197, CFPB Circular 2022-03).
- Acknowledged practitioner feedback (Neil P. Osnato, Persistence Analytics Group LLC) as professional feedback, not endorsement.
- Updated the CSV (50 questions) and published the PDF of version 1.3.

## 1.2 — November 2026
- Refined questions 4 (shared dependencies across critical providers), 9 (scope, coverage period and exceptions of security reports), 31 (who can accept a material unresolved issue, on what evidence, until when), 35 (recovery measured against the institution's own RTO and RPO) and 38 (trigger to revisit the risk assessment after material change).
- Added a note to "How to use it": a control that exists is not the same as evidence you can rely on.
- Added practitioner feedback and acknowledgments (Neil P. Osnato, Persistence Analytics Group LLC).
