# Vendor Risk Checklist for Community Banks and Credit Unions

A free, open checklist of **45 practical questions** to help community banks and credit unions select, contract, oversee and exit their technology providers (core processing, digital banking, payments).

It turns the U.S. interagency third-party risk guidance into plain questions, organized by the six stages of a provider relationship:

| Stage | Questions |
|---|---|
| 1. Planning | 1–8 |
| 2. Due diligence and selection | 9–17 |
| 3. Contract | 18–27 |
| 4. Oversight and accountability | 28–32 |
| 5. Ongoing monitoring | 33–39 |
| 6. Termination and exit | 40–45 |

## Why it exists

Small institutions depend on a few technology providers, and one provider failure can hit many institutions at once. In late 2023, a ransomware attack on a single technology provider (it began on November 26, 2023, according to the NCUA) disrupted services at roughly 60 credit unions. Large banks have dedicated vendor-management teams; most community banks and credit unions do not.

## How to use it

1. Use it before signing, at renewal, and once a year for your highest-risk providers.
2. A "no" is not a failure: it is a gap to discuss with the provider, document, and track until closed.
3. Scale the effort to the potential harm: not every provider needs every question.
