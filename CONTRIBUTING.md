# How to suggest a change

Thank you for helping improve this checklist.

- **Missing question, wrong wording, or outdated source?** Open an issue and describe the change and why it matters for a community bank or credit union.
- **Tell us how you used it** (anonymously is fine): institution type and size range, which stage you used it for, and what you found. This helps prioritize the next version.
- Please do not share confidential information about your institution or your providers.

Changes are recorded in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).
